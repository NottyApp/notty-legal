Privacy Policy for Notty

Effective date: October 14, 2026

Notty is a personal productivity application for creating and managing tasks, notes, calendar events, reminders, attachments, and voice input.

We designed Notty to keep most of your personal content on your device.

1. Information stored on your device

Notty may store the following information locally on your device:

  • tasks and task details;
  • notes and note content;
  • calendar events;
  • categories and settings;
  • recurrence information;
  • completion and deletion history;
  • attachments added to notes;
  • application preferences and usage limits.

This information is stored locally by the app and is not uploaded by Notty to our own servers.

At this time, Notty does not require a Notty account and does not operate its own cloud backend for your personal planner data.

2. Voice input and speech recognition

Notty can use your microphone and Apple speech recognition technologies to convert spoken input into text.

Microphone and speech recognition access is used only after you grant the relevant iOS permissions.

Depending on your device, operating system, language, and Apple’s speech recognition implementation, audio or speech data may be processed by Apple and may not always be processed exclusively on your device.

Notty does not intentionally store raw microphone recordings as part of normal voice input.

Recognized text may be stored locally when you choose to create a task, event, note, or dictate text into a note.

You can revoke microphone or speech recognition access at any time in iOS Settings.

3. Photos, camera and files

If you choose to attach photos, camera images, or files to a note, Notty accesses only the content you select or create through the relevant system interface.

Attachments used by Notty may be copied into the app’s local storage so that they remain available inside your notes.

Notty does not upload these attachments to our own servers.

You can remove attachments or delete the associated note from within the app.

4. Notifications

Notty may request permission to send local notifications for reminders.

Reminder scheduling is performed locally on your device.

Notty does not currently use remote push notifications for planner reminders.

You can disable notifications at any time in iOS Settings.

5. Feedback and support

If you voluntarily send feedback or contact support, you may provide:

  • your email address, if you choose to include it;
  • your feedback message;
  • an optional attachment;
  • limited technical diagnostic information such as the app version, build number, iOS version, and device model.

Feedback is sent using your device’s available sharing or email functionality to:

nottyapp@proton.me

Information you voluntarily send by email is processed by the email providers involved in delivering the message.

We use this information only to respond to support requests, investigate problems, and improve Notty.

6. Analytics, advertising and tracking

The current version of Notty does not intentionally use advertising networks or cross-app tracking.

Notty does not sell your personal information.

If analytics, crash reporting, cloud synchronization, or other third-party services are added in the future, this Privacy Policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated accordingly.

7. Purchases

The current beta version of Notty may display information about planned paid features or subscription options.

Purchases may not yet be available during beta testing.

If Apple In-App Purchases are enabled in a future release, payment processing will be handled by Apple. Notty will not receive your full payment-card information.

8. Data retention and deletion

Planner data stored by Notty remains on your device until you delete it through the app, remove the application, or otherwise erase the relevant device data.

Some locally managed files may temporarily remain after actions such as Undo in order to prevent accidental data loss.

If you send us feedback or support email, those messages may remain in the relevant email system for as long as reasonably necessary to handle the request and maintain support records.

You may request deletion of information you previously sent directly to us by contacting:

nottyapp@proton.me

9. Permissions and your choices

You can manage permissions such as microphone, speech recognition, photos, camera, and notifications through iOS Settings.

Notty may lose access to related functionality when a permission is revoked, but the rest of the application can continue to function where technically possible.

10. Children

Notty is not specifically designed or marketed for children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through our own servers.

11. Security

We take reasonable measures to limit unnecessary data collection and keep planner content local where possible.

However, no device, software, or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Notty changes, including when new features, cloud services, analytics, purchases, or third-party integrations are introduced.

The updated policy will be published at this same public URL with a revised effective date.

13. Contact

For privacy questions, support, or deletion requests:

nottyapp@proton.me
